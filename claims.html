Claims & interoperability

The practice sees outcomes. The payer platform sees X12.

Cloud Dental Office is the practice surface. Cloud Health Office owns payer connectivity and claim intelligence. Staff see lifecycle status, a timeline, patient responsibility, and posted financials — not clearinghouse vendors.

Sister platform

Cloud Health Office for payers.

Together they close provider ↔ payer interoperability. The browser never calls Cloud Health Office. The portal does.

CloudDentalOffice
  → submit claim to CloudHealthOffice
  → GET /api/claims/{claimId}/intelligence
  → practice-facing lifecycle
     (status, timeline, patient responsibility)
  → post billed / insurance / contractual amounts
     to the patient ledger

Configure CloudHealthOffice BaseUrl, IntelligencePath, and Enabled. Override the base URL when the payer platform uses a different namespace.
