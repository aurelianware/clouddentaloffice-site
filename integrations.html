Integrations

Other services connect to your schedule. They don’t replace it.

Cloud Dental Office holds the calendar, the patient record, and the balance. Other services connect at the edges. Here is what connects today and how.

Pilot · October 2026

Zocdoc

New patients book through Zocdoc and land on your schedule.

Cloud Dental Office connects through Zocdoc’s scheduling API. Your open times on Zocdoc come from the same calendar your staff work from, the same one your website booking reads. When a time is booked or blocked in the office, it comes off Zocdoc. When a patient books on Zocdoc, the appointment comes back to your schedule with the patient attached.

Confirmation: Zocdoc bookings are confirmed automatically. Before an appointment is confirmed, the time is re-checked against your calendar; if it’s no longer open, the booking isn’t made. Unlike website requests, your staff don’t approve each Zocdoc booking first.

Zocdoc has agreed to a pilot of this integration starting in October 2026, and 3rd Set Smiles is the first dental practice in it. We’re inviting more practices already listed on Zocdoc to join.

Join the Zocdoc pilot →

Not on Zocdoc yet? A Zocdoc referral link for new practices is coming at launch.

What’s shared with Zocdoc

  • Sees: the open times you publish, by provider, location, and visit type.
  • Doesn’t see: your patient records, charts, or other appointments.
  • Cancellations and reschedules stay in step both ways.
  • If the connection to Zocdoc is interrupted, your schedule keeps working.
Ready

Your practice website

A “Book online” button that shows real open times.

Your website shows open times from your calendar. A patient picks one and sends a request. Your staff see it in Appointment Requests, match the patient, and approve it. If the booking service can’t take a request, it says so right away, so your website can fall back to email instead of losing it.

For your web developer

The availability and booking-request APIs are documented on Scheduling APIs.

Ready

Cloud Health Office

Our sister platform for claims.

Claims go out through Cloud Health Office, built by the same team. What comes back is the claim’s status, a timeline, the patient’s portion, and the payment, posted to the ledger. Your staff never see payer file formats or clearinghouse names.

In progress

Stedi

Eligibility checks, sent to the payer.

When your staff check a patient’s coverage, the request goes to the payer through Stedi, a healthcare clearinghouse, and the answer comes back to the patient’s record. Each practice gets its own Stedi account, and its key is kept in a secure vault and used only for that practice.

Being set up practice by practice for the pilot.

What your staff see

Coverage, deductibles, and maximums in plain language. Clearinghouse names and payer file formats stay out of the way.

Stedi is a separate company. Using its APIs is not an endorsement by Stedi.

In progress

Stripe

Patients pay their balance online, into your own account.

Your practice connects its own Stripe account. Patients pay on Stripe’s hosted checkout page, and the money settles in your account, not ours. The payment posts to the patient’s ledger only after Stripe confirms it. Card numbers never touch Cloud Dental Office.

Built and tested in Stripe’s test environment. The first live payments come with the pilot.

Your account, your payouts

Stripe onboards the practice directly and pays out to your bank. Turning off online payments in Cloud Dental Office doesn’t close your Stripe account.

Stripe is a separate company. Using its payment APIs is not an endorsement by Stripe.
