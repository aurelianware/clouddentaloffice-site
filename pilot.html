Pilot · October 2026

New patients from Zocdoc, straight onto your schedule.

Zocdoc has agreed to pilot the Cloud Dental Office integration starting in October 2026. 3rd Set Smiles is the first dental practice in it, and we’re looking for more.

Your open times on Zocdoc come from the same calendar your front desk uses. When a patient books on Zocdoc, the time is re-checked against your calendar and the appointment lands on your schedule with the patient attached. No second calendar to keep in sync.

The Zocdoc pilot is for practices already listed on Zocdoc. It runs on Cloud Dental Office as your schedule, so practices in it join the founding-practice pilot too. The founding-practice pilot itself is open to any practice, on Zocdoc or not.

A good fit if

  • You list on Zocdoc today.
  • You want Zocdoc bookings on the same calendar your staff already use.
  • You’ll tell us what works and what doesn’t during the pilot.

What founding practices get

More attention than a normal customer gets.

Hands-on onboarding

We set it up with your team, at your office’s pace. You aren’t handed a login and a help center.

A direct line to the founder

Questions and problems go to the person who built it. No ticket queue, no call center.

Roadmap influence

What you need moves up the list. The in-progress and reserved items get built in the order founding practices need them.

What we ask

Two things.

Honest feedback

Tell us what is slow, confusing, or missing, while it is still easy to change. The software is shaped by what happens at your front desk.

A case study, if it goes well

If the pilot works for your office, we ask to tell the story, with your approval of every word. If it doesn’t, we don’t.
