About

A career on the payer side of the claim. Now on the dentist’s side.

Founder

Mark Phillips

Mark has spent more than 25 years building healthcare payer core systems: the claims engines that insurers run. That work includes TriZetto/QNXT and Molina, and he is a co-inventor on the MyQNXT patent.

That work sits on the payer side, in the software that receives a claim, checks it, and decides what gets paid. After a career there, you learn why claims get denied.

Cloud Dental Office points that knowledge the other way. It is software that helps dentists get paid.
