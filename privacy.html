Privacy

This marketing site stores almost nothing.

The product platform is a separate system, with its own intake and PHI boundaries. This page covers clouddental.io only.

What this site collects

The contact form sends your name, practice, email, and message through Formspree, a form-processing service, which delivers it to sales@cloudhealthoffice.com. Formspree keeps a copy of each submission; this site itself stores nothing. If the form cannot send, it opens your mail client instead. We keep the correspondence in order to reply.

What this site does not do

No accounts. No tracking pixels we control. No sale of personal information. Do not send PHI, patient names, member IDs, or clinical notes to the marketing inbox.

The product

Cloud Dental Office the software handles healthcare information under a different operating model. See Trust and the product SECURITY.md. Visitor-submitted booking intake in a deployed instance must be treated as sensitive healthcare information.
