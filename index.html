Where the money leaks

The dentistry is done. The paperwork isn’t.

Most offices don’t lose money in the operatory. They lose it at the front desk, days or weeks later.

Eligibility surprises

The patient is already in the chair when someone learns the plan lapsed, or the frequency limit is used up.

Denied claims

A claim comes back weeks later needing information. Someone reworks it by hand, and the money waits.

Double entry

The patient, the appointment, the claim, the payment. Typed into more than one system that doesn’t talk to the others.

Chasing ERAs

The payer paid. Now someone finds the remittance and posts it line by line to the right patient.

Empty chairs

New patients look for an opening at 9 p.m. Your schedule isn’t where they are looking.

How it works

One record, from the booking to the payment.

Enter the patient once. The schedule, the claim, and the balance all come from that record. Each step is marked with where it stands today.

  1. Ready

    1 · Book

    New patients choose an open time on your website and it arrives as a request on your schedule. Your staff match the patient and approve it. Zocdoc booking is in progress.

  2. In progress

    2 · Check coverage

    Verify eligibility before the visit, not after the claim comes back. The screens and workflow are built. Live payer connections are the remaining work.

  3. Ready

    3 · Claim

    Build the claim from the visit you already recorded and submit it. Follow it in plain language: submitted, accepted, information needed, denied, paid. Each status shows the next step.

  4. Ready

    4 · Get paid

    When the payer pays, the insurance payment and adjustment post to the patient’s ledger. Nobody retypes it. Online card payments for the patient’s share are in progress.
